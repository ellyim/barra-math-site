Meet Barra

Barra is a friendly bar-model character who turns everyday moments — grocery trips, sharing snacks, building block towers — into number sense practice. One consistent character makes math feel like a story, not a worksheet.

21 Levels, One Growing Skill

Every level keeps the same story and structure — only the numbers grow, from counting within 10 up to bar models within 40.

Sample: Counting at the Market

Each level includes 8 pages — counting, comparison, number bonds, sharing, and a full bar model addition & subtraction introduction.
