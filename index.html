Frequently Asked Questions

What age is Barra Math for?

Barra Math worksheets are designed for children ages 5–6 (Pre-K and Kindergarten), with a 21-level curriculum that grows in difficulty.

What is the bar model method?

The bar model method is a visual math strategy, popularized by Singapore Math, that represents numbers as rectangular bars so children can see part-whole relationships and solve addition, subtraction, and word problems visually before moving to abstract equations.

How many worksheets are included in Barra Math?

Each Barra Math level includes 8 printable pages. There are 21 levels total, with numbers progressing from within 10 at Level 1 up to within 40 at Level 21.

Is Barra Math affiliated with Singapore Math?

No. Barra Math is an independent, original worksheet series inspired by the visual bar-model teaching approach popularized by Singapore Math. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any specific curriculum brand.
